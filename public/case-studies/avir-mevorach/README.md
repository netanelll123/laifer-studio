# Avir Mevorach — case study assets

All real, all in place — no placeholders on this page.

## Assets
- `hero.mp4` / `hero-poster.jpg` — the boy (helmet off) flying over a forest at
  golden hour, then standing on a flower-covered hilltop. Sourced from a real
  8s AI-generated clip, re-encoded (H.264, muted, faststart) for web delivery.
- `scene-city-crowd.jpg` — establishing shot: the boy in his diving helmet,
  alone in a crowded vintage city street.
- `scene-helmet-closeup.jpg` — close-up on the helmet, steam venting, the city
  reflected in the glass.
- `scene-helmet-lift.jpg` — the boy lifting the helmet off, warm light
  spilling out. (A lower-resolution, watermarked duplicate of this shot was
  received too — this is the cleaned-up, higher-res version; the duplicate
  was discarded.)
- `scene-breathing.jpg` — the completed reveal: helmet off, eyes closed,
  breathing in golden-hour light.
- `scene-meadow-run.jpg` — the boy running joyfully through a flower field,
  representing the world "filling with color" once his perception changes.
- `film-poster.jpg` — a second, distinct frame from the same source clip
  (the mid-air flying moment), used only for the video-embed thumbnail so it
  doesn't repeat `hero-poster.jpg`.
